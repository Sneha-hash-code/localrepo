About

Building things that matter, one commit at a time

I'm a full-stack developer who loves turning ideas into working products — from a ride-sharing platform for my hometown to an artisan marketplace that gives local craftspeople a storefront online. I care about clean architecture, thoughtful UI, and code that other people can actually read.

Lately I'm deep into artificial intelligence — experimenting with prompt engineering, AI-assisted tooling, and how AI can genuinely make everyday products smarter rather than just adding a chatbot for the sake of it. I'm just as energized by the problem-solving grind of hackathons as I am by shipping polished side projects and documenting the journey for other beginners.

  • Web Development — building fast, accessible, full-stack products
  • Artificial Intelligence — prompt engineering & AI-powered features
  • Problem Solving — debugging, algorithms, and hackathon sprints
  • Impactful Products — tools built for real, local use cases
  • Continuous Learning — a new stack or concept every few months
